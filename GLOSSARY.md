# Glossary

## Almost Ready

A customer-facing state or notification indicating the party should prepare to return soon, but the table is not ready yet.

## Assigned Table

The table selected for a queue entry. In MVP this is staff-confirmed.

## Call Next

Staff action that moves a waiting party into a called state and triggers customer notification.

## Customer

The diner or party representative who joins the waitlist.

## Dynamic ETA

An estimated wait time that adjusts based on queue state, party size, table availability, historical wait data, and turnover patterns.

## ETA

Estimated time until the party is likely to be seated or called.

## Host

The staff member managing the waitlist and seating decisions during service.

## "I'm On My Way"

Customer action that tells the restaurant the party is returning after being notified.

## No-Show

A queue entry where the customer does not arrive after being called or within the configured grace period.

## Party

The group of diners represented by one queue entry.

## Party Size

Number of diners in the party. This is central to table matching.

## Party-To-Table Matching

The process of choosing the best table for a waiting party based on party size, table capacity, status, section, wait time, and operational rules.

## Queue Entry

One record in the restaurant waitlist representing a waiting party.

## QR Check-In

Customer flow where a diner scans a QR code and joins the waitlist through a mobile web page.

## Rejoin Queue

A later-stage feature allowing a customer to re-enter the waitlist after cancellation, missed call, or no-show according to restaurant rules.

## Rush Mode

A high-demand operating mode for peak periods. It can emphasize longest waits, table utilization, notification cadence, queue capacity, and staff warnings.

## Seating Event

An auditable event representing a queue, table, notification, or seating state transition.

## Section

A physical area of the restaurant, such as indoor, outdoor, family, bar, or private dining.

## Staff Dashboard

The live operational interface used by hosts and restaurant staff to manage queue entries, table status, and seating.

## Table Combining

Joining multiple physical tables to seat a larger party.

## Table Ready

Notification/state indicating a table is ready and the customer should return immediately.

## Table Turnover

The time between seating a party and the table becoming available again after dining and cleaning.

## Transactional WhatsApp

Operational WhatsApp communication related to queue status, table readiness, cancellation, or reminders. This is distinct from promotional marketing.

## Waitlist

The active ordered set of parties waiting to be seated.
