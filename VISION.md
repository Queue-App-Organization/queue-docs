## Product Thesis

We are not digitizing the queue. We are digitizing the restaurant's seating operation.

The product should become a WhatsApp-first digital waitlist and table management system for Indian restaurants. Its main value is reducing waiting-room chaos for restaurants while making wait time feel clear, fair, and predictable for customers.

## Core Problem

Busy Indian restaurants often manage walk-ins through a mix of paper lists, memory, phone calls, ad hoc WhatsApp messages, and staff judgment. This creates avoidable problems:

- Customers do not know when to return.
- Hosts struggle to match the right party to the right table.
- Owners lack visibility into wait time, no-shows, lost demand, and table utilization.
- Peak-hour operations depend too heavily on individual staff experience.

## Product Promise

For customers:

> Join quickly, wait remotely, receive timely WhatsApp updates, and arrive when the table is actually close to ready.

For restaurant staff:

> See demand, predict wait, match parties to tables, call customers, seat them, and keep tables moving.

For owners:

> Understand operational bottlenecks and improve table utilization without forcing staff into complex software.

## Target Positioning

Do not position this as generic "queue management software." The sharper category is:

> WhatsApp-first waitlist and seating operations for Indian restaurants.

## Core Loops

Customer loop:

```text
JOIN -> WAIT REMOTELY -> GET NOTIFIED -> ARRIVE -> MATCH TABLE -> SEAT
```

Restaurant loop:

```text
SEE DEMAND -> PREDICT WAIT -> MATCH PARTY -> CALL -> SEAT -> FREE TABLE -> REPEAT
```

## Strategic Focus

The first product should solve one operational problem exceptionally well:

> Reduce perceived waiting for customers while helping staff seat the right party at the right table at the right time.

## Long-Term Direction

The long-term opportunity is to move from waitlist tooling into a restaurant operating engine:

- Smart ETA.
- Party-to-table matching.
- Table turnover prediction.
- Recommended next action for hosts.
- Reservation and walk-in optimization.
- Multi-location operational analytics.

## Boundaries

Build the wedge around:

- Waiting.
- Communication.
- Seating.
- Table status.
- Basic operational analytics.

Do not initially build:

- Ordering.
- Payments.
- POS.
- Inventory.
- Kitchen display systems.
- Delivery.
- Loyalty.
- Native customer app.
- Facial recognition.
- Physical token machines.
- Enterprise integration marketplace.

## Founder Role

The founder/product owner should focus on:

- What business are we building?
- Who is the customer?
- What does success look like?
- What should we never build?
- What market do we want to own?

The AI/software organization can handle decomposition, tickets, design, architecture, implementation, QA, deployment, analytics, and iteration, but high-risk product and business decisions need explicit human approval.
